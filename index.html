Business process

Customer journey

StepStageWhat happens
1BrowseViews the static catalogues of sellers. Free.
2EnquirePlaces an enquiry with Delper to avail independent 3PQC and the RTB. No separate fee.
3Compare bidsVetted sellers bid. Bids are ranked by verified QC pass rate and inventory data, not ad spend.
4Choose and paySelects a bid and pays the all-inclusive price into escrow. Taxes and FX conversion are charged extra at checkout.
5Inspect3PQC inspects the order and issues the sign-off code, clearing it for dispatch.
6ReceiveGoods are delivered to the doorstep. The customer issues a satisfaction certificate within 15 days, which releases escrow.
7ReturnVerified sellers and protected payments bring the customer back for repeat purchases.

Seller journey

StepStageWhat happens
1ListLists a static catalogue free, with no onboarding fee.
2OnboardPays the USD 100 annual business onboarding fee and is certified as a Healthy Seller.
3Agree termsAgrees to RTB participation, with lead generation deducted from sale proceeds only on escrow release.
4BidReceives enquiries and bids in the RTB. RTB and QC costs are prepaid at actual cost, non-refundable.
5FulfilWins the order and produces or prepares the goods.
6Pass QC3PQC rates the product. Passing products get the sign-off code and RTB visibility. Low-rated products get no further QC or RTB expense and stay listed with their rating.
7Get paidAfter delivery and the satisfaction certificate, escrow is released. The 2% commission and lead generation are deducted and the balance is paid.
8RepeatReturning customers place new enquiries, and lead generation supports repeat sales.

Escrow is released only after delivery and the customer's satisfaction certificate, due within 15 days. Buyer acquisition runs through ONDC in India only, as proposed, with Axis Bank escrow in progress.

Revenue generation breakup

Each user buys one USD 600 order a year. Delper earns a 2% commission (USD 12.00) plus USD 66.67 of lead generation, deducted from seller proceeds at escrow release, so gross profit is USD 78.67 per user a year (13.1% of the order value). Figures are gross profit, before Delper's own taxes and expenses.

Notes

  • Retail take rate: 13.1% of the USD 600 order (USD 78.67 gross profit per order: USD 12.00 commission plus USD 66.67 lead generation).
  • Each user buys one USD 600 order a year, giving USD 78.67 gross profit per user. Year 10 at 6B buyers means every user in the base buys, which is a stretch case. It is possible but should be treated as an upside case.
  • ONDC reports about 500,000 sellers, or 4.1% of India's scaled dataset vendors. Use the ONDC figure for the pilot and the dataset figure for the market ceiling.
  • Users are 6B for both retail and bulk, as agreed. The lead-gen calibration stays at USD 66.67 per user, so the pool at this base is USD 400B. Internet users and vendors are scaled country by country to match (users by 1.1958 where penetration allows, vendors in tandem).
