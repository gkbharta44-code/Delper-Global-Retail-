Executive Summary: 5-Year Hyper-Scale Track

The Thesis

Delper is a buyer-first verified sourcing platform designed to eliminate transaction risks—such as counterfeit goods, phantom stock, and non-delivery—by establishing a neutral trust layer across 195 countries.

To capture the global e-commerce landscape, Delper is executing a 5-Year Hyper-Scale Track targeting 6 billion onboarded users by Year 5. By embedding its verification and escrow protocols natively into global payment processors, checkout engines, and e-commerce infrastructure, Delper scales demand without direct customer acquisition friction.

📉 Core Problem & The Delper Fix

  • The Problem: Global online shoppers and B2B buyers lose billions annually to fraud, misrepresentation, and upfront payment defaults. Existing marketplace assurance tools are fragmented, biased toward ad spend, and locked into specific ecosystems.
  • The Fix: A cross-platform, decentralized trust protocol where buyers pay absolutely zero fees. Sellers prepay bidding and quality control costs at exact actual costs and agree to transactional commission/lead-generation deductions only upon successful escrow release.

🔄 The Hyper-Scale Operational Workflow

Stage Protocol Execution
1. Browse & Enquire Buyers view free static seller catalogs and post product enquiries, generating highly qualified leads.
2. Algorithmic RTB Vetted factories bid live. Visibility and rankings are driven strictly by verified Third-Party Quality Control (3PQC) pass rates and inventory data, completely eliminating ad-spend manipulation.
3. Conditional Escrow The buyer pays the all-inclusive price into a secure regional escrow account.
4. Programmatic 3PQC High-risk or sampled batches undergo physical inspection by accredited partners (e.g., SGS, Bureau Veritas). Low-rated goods ship only if the buyer signs an un-rejectable legal caveat waiver.
5. Auto-Release Payout Upon delivery, a 15-day satisfaction window is triggered. If no dispute is raised, funds auto-release to the seller, minus Delper’s 2% commission and calibrated lead-generation fees.
